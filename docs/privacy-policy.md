# 足印日記 隱私權政策 · Pawprint Days Privacy Policy

_Draft · last updated 2026-11-23 · host this page at a public URL and enter it in App Store Connect → App Information → Privacy Policy URL._

## 繁體中文

**足印日記不需要帳號，也沒有我們自己的伺服器。** 你的日記、毛孩、步數幣與散步軌跡都只存在你的手機上。

**我們使用哪些資料**
- **精準位置**：用來在真實街角遇見毛孩、計算 75 公尺街角距離並繪製散步軌跡。只在手機上處理，不會上傳。
- **步數（計步器／動作與健身）**：用來換算步數幣與口袋拾遺進度。只在手機上處理，不會上傳。
- **日記與遊戲進度**：以 SwiftData 與 UserDefaults 儲存在本機。你主動匯出（PDF／備份）時，檔案才會透過系統分享選單交給你選擇的對象。
- **通知**：若你開啟提醒，只會在本機排程，不經過任何伺服器。

**即時天氣（第三方服務：Open-Meteo）**
為了讓小窩的天氣跟現實同步，App 會把**四捨五入到約 1 公里的模糊座標**傳送到 Open-Meteo（open-meteo.com）取得當地天氣代碼，最多每 30 分鐘一次。我們不會傳送你的精準位置、姓名或任何裝置識別碼；如同一般網路請求，Open-Meteo 會看到你的 IP 位址，其處理方式請參閱 Open-Meteo 的隱私說明。無法連線時，App 會改用手機時間推算光影。

**我們不做的事**：不追蹤、不投放廣告、不使用第三方分析或廣告 SDK、不出售或分享你的資料、不將資料與你的身分連結。

**兒童**：本 App 不會蒐集可識別個人的資料。

**變更**：若政策有變，我們會更新本頁日期並於 App 更新說明中告知。

## English

**Pawprint Days has no accounts and no servers of its own.** Your diary, companions, step coins and stroll trails live only on your phone.

**Data we use**
- **Precise location** — to meet companions at real street corners, measure the 75 m corner distance and draw your stroll trail. Processed on device only; never uploaded.
- **Steps (pedometer / Motion & Fitness)** — to earn step coins and pocket-find progress. Processed on device only; never uploaded.
- **Diary and game progress** — stored locally (SwiftData, UserDefaults). Exports (PDF / backup) leave the app only when you tap export and choose where to share them.
- **Notifications** — if you opt in, they are scheduled locally on your device; no server is involved.

**Live weather (third-party service: Open-Meteo)**
So the weather in your Sunroom matches the real world, the app sends **coordinates rounded to about 1 km** to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to retrieve a local weather code, at most once every 30 minutes. We do not send your precise location, name or any device identifier. As with any web request, Open-Meteo can see your IP address; see Open-Meteo's own privacy information for how it handles that. If the request fails or you are offline, the app falls back to lighting based on the phone's clock.

**What we don't do:** no tracking, no ads, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, no selling or sharing of your data, nothing linked to your identity.

**Children:** the app does not collect personally identifiable information.

**Changes:** if this policy changes we will update the date above and mention it in the app's release notes.
